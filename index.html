Next Step Equity Group was founded with a simple belief: every homeowner deserves a fair, honest solution — no matter their situation. What started as a mission to help families facing foreclosure has grown into a full-service home buying company serving homeowners across Maryland.

His mission is simple: provide transparent, fair offers and treat every homeowner like family. With deep roots in Maryland's communities, Jay and his team have helped hundreds of families turn their toughest real estate challenges into fresh starts.
